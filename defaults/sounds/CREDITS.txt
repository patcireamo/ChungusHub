They came from the Moodist project (https://github.com/remvze/moodist), which
states that the sounds it ships are under either the Pixabay Content License or
CC0 1.0 Universal. That project keeps no per-sound breakdown of which is which,
so neither does this file. What is known is that every recording here is under
one of the two:

    Pixabay Content License
    https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/

    CC0 1.0 Universal
    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Neither of them asks for credit, and this notice is not one. It is here because
these files are not ChungusHub's own work, and anyone who redistributes the app
is entitled to know which terms they are passing on.

Both allow the recordings to be bundled in an application and redistributed that
way. The Pixabay license draws one line worth repeating: content may not be sold
or distributed on its own, with nothing built around it. Shipping these files
inside ChungusHub is fine; lifting them back out and selling them as a sound
pack is not.

The files keep the names they had and are copied byte for byte, with one
exception: transport/train.mp3 is cut, without re-encoding, to the steady stretch
of its source (about 4.3 s to 31.8 s), since the rest of that recording is the
train fading away into silence. Nothing was transcoded, and there was no mixing
or level change, so every other file runs exactly as long as its source and all
of them sound exactly as they were published. The set is
a subset of that project's: recordings that did not suit a roleplay workspace
were left behind rather than altered. The folder is the list, so a sound added
or dropped needs no edit here.
